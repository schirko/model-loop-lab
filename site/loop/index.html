The loop works

A small evolutionary search hunts for better forest-cover models. Each generation it builds ten candidate models, scores them on plots they never saw, keeps the top three and makes new candidates by changing their settings a little.

  1. BuildTen models with different settings: model type, number of trees, tree depth, leaf size.
  2. ScoreEach one predicts forest cover on held-out plots. Accuracy is the score.
  3. KeepThe three best survive. The other seven are dropped.
  4. ChangeThe survivors are copied with small random changes to make the next ten.

Then back to step 1, for 20 generations. No person picks the settings along the way.
